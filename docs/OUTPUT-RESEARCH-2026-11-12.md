# Topic and output tracking: research and a lean plan (2026-11-12)

Five search-only agy runs (topic and intent tracking, output tracking, spoken output design, lean architecture and hooks, evaluation), the owner's sessions of 09:55 and 10:26 as the case, and our code. Raw reports and briefs: `.scratch/output-research/` (git-ignored). The reports converge strongly (the same six ideas lead all five); their line counts and thresholds (Jaccard 0.55, 600 ms, 25 s) are their guesses, not measurements. `conversation.item.truncate` (OpenAI Realtime) and Pipecat's context-aggregator placement are real mechanisms; the exact API quotes were not re-fetched (the OpenAI page is script-rendered).

## What happens today, in numbers (`scripts/output_audit.py`, new, free)
Over the four agent-server logs of 2026-11-12 (105 turns): **6** replies with two first-person status sentences that restate each other, **3** premature claims ("I have updated ..." in a turn that only started a job), **9** delegate calls within 25 s of another, **4** one- or two-word turns that started a lookup or job ("Mm-hmm." -> web_answer, "I'm sorry." -> delegate; "Title page" -> delegate is a legitimate refinement). ## What the research says, against what we already have
| Idea (agy) | We have | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Truncate the history to what was heard after a barge-in | the client adds the sentences begun so far (`history.add(question, spoken)`) | **have** at sentence level; word-level needs TTS word timestamps: skip |
| Defer the "say" sentence and drop it if the tool answers fast | `SAY_DELAY_S` 0.6 s; slow tools (weather, web_answer, search_docs, delegate) speak it at once, measured 1.3 s or more | **have**; the visible repeat is only that the say names the subject the answer names again: natural, not changed |
| Two-slot topic register (task vs side topic) | chain + side-question neutrality + finished task stays open (section 19) | **have** |
| Cancel and replace on a repair ("sorry, I mean ...") | merge of an overlapping job, same deliverable, follow-up queue; but a restated request with other wording and another folder starts a second and third job | **new**, high value |
| Drop perfect-tense claims while a job has only started | nothing | **new**, small |
| Restatement filter between sentences of one reply (say, then "OK, I am ...") | payload and source filters only | **new**, medium |
| Backchannel and fragment gate before any tool | `is_acknowledgement` guards delegate only | **extend** to every tool |
| Notice dedupe against what was just said | notices wait for a quiet moment (0.8 s) | **new**, small |

## Plan (each under about 40 lines; deterministic or one prompt line)
1. **Restated request replaces the running job** (problems 2, 6): a repair marker in the user's words ("sorry, I mean", "no, I mean", "I meant") plus a job started in the last 30 s cancels it; the new job starts and one fixed sentence is spoken; two delegate calls with the same normalised task inside 25 s are one.
2. **Claim guard** (3): in the turn that started a job, perfect-tense completion sentences ("I have updated/added/created ...") are dropped from the spoken stream, plus one prompt line (progressive tense until a result exists).
3. **Restatement filter** (2): within one reply, a first-person status sentence that overlaps the one before it (the audit's rule) is dropped; the job-start sentence is built from code, not by the model.
4. **Backchannel gate** (7): "mm-hmm", "uh-huh", "I'm sorry", "okay" and similar closed-class turns start no tool and no model call (unless the last assistant turn ended in an offer).
5. **Notice dedupe** (9): a finished-job notice that repeats most of what was said in the last minute is shortened to "Done.".
Not recommended: embedding or NLI similarity, an LLM judge for redundancy, per-word truncation, a second always-on model.

## Caveats
The audit counts are on 105 turns from four sessions, not a benchmark; the thresholds must be tuned on logs, not taken from the reports. Cutting sentences risks cutting content: the filters only touch first-person status sentences. 

## Built (2026-11-12, later)
1. **Restated request replaces the running job** (`agent_tools.replace_running_job`): "I mean" in the user's words plus a job started in the last 30 s cancels that job, the new job starts, and the spoken sentence is "Okay, I've switched to that. I'll tell you when it's done." While testing it live a race showed up in the delegate server: a job cancelled a moment earlier was still counted as running while its task unwound, so the replacement was queued behind it and lost (the model then said it was being created). The server now counts and merges only jobs whose status is running. Live scenario `restated_request_replaces_job`: 2 of 3 before that fix, **6 of 6** after.
2. **Claim guard**, 3. **restatement filter** (`voice_pipeline/spoken_filter.py`, `SentenceFilter` in `agent_server.handle`): a sentence that opens like a status sentence ("I am ...", "OK, I ...", "It's ...", "The ...") is held until it ends and dropped if it restates an earlier one of the reply (the `say` sentence counts) or, in a turn that started a job, claims completion ("I have updated ..."); every other sentence streams through at once. The log keeps what was spoken and the dropped sentences (`dropped_sentences`). Over the socket and unit tests; not exercisable by the live runner (it bypasses the server's stream).
4. **Backchannel gate**: "mm-hmm", "uh-huh", "hmm", "I see", "sorry", "I'm sorry", "oops" on their own start no model call and get no reply, unless the last assistant turn was an offer ("uh-huh" may then mean yes) or the task waits for an answer. "okay", "yes", "sure" are not in the set (they answer offers).
5. **Notice dedupe** (client, `doc_chat/notices.py`): a finished-job notice whose content words were mostly said in the last 60 s becomes "That's done.".
Also: the delegate result is recorded in the turn log (`result`), and `delegate`'s task comparison ignores the boilerplate sentences the harness adds (manifest request, update sentence, keep-all list): the manifest request had made "a snake game" and "a page about lighthouses" look like one task. Known and left: when a second, unrelated page is asked for while a job runs ("Also make a page about X") the model writes one task for both, and the server runs it as a queued continuation (both get built, one after the other; `independent_builds` fails 0 of 3 on that, it passed 2 of 2 before); `repeat_while_running` still queues one continuation job for a reworded repeat.
Run `scripts/output_audit.py` on the next live session's `pipeline/out/agent_server/*.jsonl` and compare with the baseline above (6, 3, 9, 4).

## Built: "say separate" (2026-11-12, last)
For an unrelated request while a job runs ("Also make a page about X" next to a snake game) the owner chose the cheap way: no question. The server still queues an overlapping request as an addition, and the agent says one fixed sentence, built in code (`QUEUED_SAY`): "I'll add that after the first one finishes. Say separate to start it now." If the user says separate (or "start it now"), the model calls `delegate` again with only the new request and `separate=true`: the server then starts it beside the running job, drops the addition that was queued (it would have run after the first job and redone its work), and the harness adds no "update the existing page" sentence. The `delegate` tool description now also says to give each unrelated request its own call and to write only that request in `task`; that removed most of the cases where the model wrote one task for both. Live: `independent_builds` 4 of 5 pass (was 0 of 3 to 0 of 4 before the description change; one run still folds both requests into one independent job, one is queued with the fixed sentence), `separate_on_request` (snake game, "also a page about X", "Separate.") 4 of 5. Not solved: the model sometimes still writes one combined task, and answers "Separate." oddly when nothing was queued.

## Assessment of the 12:44 session and four fixes (2026-11-12, afternoon)
The audit on that log (38 turns): restated status sentences 0 (was 6 in 105 turns), premature claims 0 (was 3), duplicate jobs 1 and fragments 1, both legitimate (two different requests nine seconds apart; a misheard "Google Forge" that was a real lookup). First sound median 2.6 s (p90 3.5 s), median reply 12 words, 8 of 29 replies interrupted. Problems found and fixed:
1. **A routed lookup lost the topic.** The router wrote `web_answer|Who is the current chairperson of the program?` and the web answered "unclear which program". A routed lookup whose question still holds a reference (he, she, they, its, "the program", "that company", ...) now goes to the model with the history (`call_policy.lookup_needs_context`, `router_reason` "the question refers back to the conversation").
2. **A garbled temperature question got a web answer about Manila** for a user in Cebu. A temperature, rain or forecast question with a now-word ("current", "right now", "today", "outside") that is not about the sun, the Pi, a CPU or a device becomes the weather tool for the user's own place, or the place named after "in" (`call_policy.weather_args`, applied in `plan`).
3. **"The Annie website" found no app** and the reply read twelve names. One distinctive word that only one app holds now opens it (`annie` -> `annie_billard`; "website", "site" and "page" no longer count in an app's name) and the spoken list stops at five ("... and 7 more").
4. **A draft's placeholder was read aloud** ("(your contact information here)"). `speakable` drops "[your name]", "(your ... here)" and "{name}" blanks. **This one needs the speech server restarted** (`make down && make up`).
Not done: the Pi cannot report its temperature (item 5 of the assessment), the say-then-refusal pair remains.
