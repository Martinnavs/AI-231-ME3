# Progress 2026-11-06 (and the evening of 11-05)

**Bottom line.** The agent harness now runs on the Pi as the brief asks (MCP client and every MCP server on the Pi;
router, speech and voice stay on the laptop). On the spoken v4 test it scores the same as the laptop harness (47 pass /
8 fail / 4 not run, against 46/8/5) and is slower: end of speech to first sound 1.63 s median untuned, 1.50 s after
tuning (laptop harness 1.30 s on AC; this laptop was on battery). The router is now `kd-web4` (cluster ticket 04).
Music plays from Spotify through the desktop app into the Pi speaker. The agent degrades gracefully on a bad or no
connection (measured with a simulated link). One serious incident: a misheard fragment deleted every app on the Pi;
jobs that delete now ask first and the workspace is snapshotted before every job.

## Incident: a misheard fragment deleted every app on the Pi

"You remove all of the files except for doll." (a cut-off piece of a longer sentence) went straight into a delegate
job that deleted all 10 app folders, including the one the owner wanted kept. Flappy Bird and Angry Birds were
restored from the laptop; the rest existed only on the Pi and are lost. Fixes: confirmation for destructive tasks,
the 1.5 s start delay, and per-job workspace snapshots (`~/agent_sandbox/jobs/snapshots/`).
