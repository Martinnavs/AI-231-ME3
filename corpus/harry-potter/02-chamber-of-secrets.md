# Book 2: Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets

During the summer Harry is visited by Dobby, a house-elf who warns him not to return to Hogwarts. When Harry refuses, Ron and his brothers rescue him from the Dursleys in a flying Ford Anglia. At school the new Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher is Gilderoy Lockhart, a vain celebrity wizard who turns out to be a fraud.

Students begin to be found petrified, frozen like statues. A message on the wall says the Chamber of Secrets has been opened and warns the enemies of the heir of Slytherin. Harry hears a voice in the walls that no one else can hear. He discovers he can speak Parseltongue, the language of snakes, which makes many students suspect him.

Harry and Ron learn from Hermione, who is later petrified herself, that a monster in the Chamber is the basilisk, a giant snake whose gaze is deadly. It has been moving through the school's pipes. Moaning Myrtle, a ghost who died in a girls' bathroom, helps them find the entrance. Hagrid is blamed and taken away because he was accused of the same crime fifty years earlier.

Harry finds an enchanted diary that belonged to a student named Tom Riddle. The diary has been controlling Ginny Weasley, Ron's younger sister. Tom Riddle is Voldemort as a teenager. Harry goes down into the Chamber to save Ginny.

Fawkes the phoenix brings Harry the Sword of Gryffindor. Harry kills the basilisk with the sword and destroys the diary with one of its fangs. Ginny wakes up. Harry also tricks Lucius Malfoy into freeing Dobby by handing Dobby a sock.
