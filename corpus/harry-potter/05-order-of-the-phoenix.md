# Book 5: Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix

The Ministry of Magic and the newspaper the Daily Prophet claim that Harry is lying about Voldemort. Harry spends the summer alone and angry. He is rescued by members of the Order of the Phoenix, a secret group led by Dumbledore, whose headquarters is Sirius Black's family home at number twelve, Grimmauld Place.

The Ministry sends Dolores Umbridge to Hogwarts as the new Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher. She becomes High Inquisitor and takes control of the school with cruel punishments and strict rules. She refuses to let students practise real defensive magic.

Hermione organizes Dumbledore's Army, a secret student group that meets in the Room of Requirement so Harry can teach defensive spells. Harry also has his first romance with Cho Chang. Dumbledore asks Professor Snape to teach Harry Occlumency, which is the skill of shielding the mind, because Harry keeps seeing visions through Voldemort's eyes.

Harry sees a vision of Sirius being tortured and rushes to the Ministry of Magic with his friends. It is a trap. In the Department of Mysteries they fight Death Eaters over a prophecy. Sirius comes to help and is killed by his cousin Bellatrix Lestrange.

Voldemort appears in public, and Dumbledore fights him. The Ministry finally admits that he has returned. Harry learns the prophecy says neither he nor Voldemort can live while the other survives.
