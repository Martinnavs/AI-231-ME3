# Book 3: Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

Sirius Black, a prisoner accused of helping Voldemort, escapes from the wizard prison Azkaban. Everyone believes he is coming to kill Harry. Guards called Dementors, creatures that drain happiness and hope, are placed around Hogwarts. Harry reacts to them badly and faints because they remind him of his parents' deaths.

The new Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher, Professor Lupin, teaches Harry the Patronus Charm to fight Dementors. Harry also gets the Marauder's Map, which shows everyone in Hogwarts. Hagrid becomes the Care of Magical Creatures teacher and introduces the class to Buckbeak the hippogriff. Hermione takes extra classes using a Time-Turner.

Harry learns that Sirius Black is his godfather. He was a friend of Harry's father and was wrongly imprisoned. The real traitor who betrayed Harry's parents was Peter Pettigrew, who faked his own death and has been hiding for twelve years as Ron's pet rat, Scabbers. Professor Lupin is a werewolf, and he was also a friend of James Potter.

In the Shrieking Shack, Sirius, Lupin, and Pettigrew are confronted. Pettigrew escapes after Harry spares his life. Buckbeak is sentenced to death. Using the Time-Turner, Harry and Hermione go back in time to save Buckbeak and help Sirius escape on his back. Harry realizes the Patronus that saved him from the Dementors was cast by himself.

Lupin resigns when his secret is revealed. Sirius remains a fugitive, but he now has a home to hope for with Harry.
