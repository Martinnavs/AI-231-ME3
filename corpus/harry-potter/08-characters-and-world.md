# Characters and the wizarding world

Harry Potter is the hero, known as the Boy Who Lived. He is brave, loyal, and often reckless. His best friends are Ron Weasley, who is funny and loyal and comes from a large red-haired family, and Hermione Granger, a brilliant Muggle-born witch who loves books and rules.

Albus Dumbledore is the wise headmaster of Hogwarts and leads the Order of the Phoenix. Rubeus Hagrid is the kind half-giant who is the gamekeeper. Severus Snape is the Potions master who seems cruel to Harry but is secretly loyal to Dumbledore. Minerva McGonagall is the strict but fair head of Gryffindor house.

Lord Voldemort, born Tom Riddle, is the villain. He wants to rule the wizarding world and live forever, and his followers are called Death Eaters. Draco Malfoy is Harry's school rival. Bellatrix Lestrange is Voldemort's most devoted and dangerous follower.

Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is a castle in Scotland with four houses: Gryffindor for the brave, Hufflepuff for the loyal, Ravenclaw for the clever, and Slytherin for the ambitious. Students are sorted into a house by the Sorting Hat. Quidditch is a sport played on flying broomsticks with four balls, and the Seeker catches the Golden Snitch to win.

Muggles are people with no magic. Muggle-born wizards have non-magical parents. Diagon Alley is the hidden shopping street in London. Platform nine and three quarters at King's Cross station is where the Hogwarts Express leaves. Common spells include Expelliarmus to disarm, Lumos for light, and Expecto Patronum to fight Dementors.
