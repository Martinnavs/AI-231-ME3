# Book 1: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Harry Potter is an orphan who lives with his unkind aunt and uncle, the Dursleys, and sleeps in the cupboard under the stairs. On his eleventh birthday the giant Rubeus Hagrid arrives and tells him he is a wizard. Harry's parents, Lily and James Potter, were killed by the dark wizard Voldemort. Harry survived the attack as a baby and was left with a lightning-shaped scar on his forehead.

Hagrid takes Harry shopping in Diagon Alley. Harry visits the wizard bank Gringotts, buys books and a cauldron, and chooses a wand at Ollivander's shop. He boards the Hogwarts Express at platform nine and three quarters. On the train he meets Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger. The Sorting Hat places Harry in the house of Gryffindor. He also meets Draco Malfoy, who becomes his rival.

At Hogwarts Harry learns that he is a talented flyer. He joins the Gryffindor Quidditch team as Seeker, the youngest in a century. The three friends rescue Hermione from a mountain troll on Halloween. They then discover that a giant three-headed dog named Fluffy is guarding a trapdoor.

Harry, Ron, and Hermione work out that Fluffy is protecting the Philosopher's Stone, a magical object made by Nicolas Flamel that gives eternal life. They believe Professor Snape wants to steal it. They get past a series of traps: the plant Devil's Snare, a room of flying keys, a life-size game of wizard chess, and a potion puzzle.

In the last chamber Harry finds Professor Quirrell, not Snape, who is carrying Voldemort on the back of his head. The Mirror of Erised hides the stone and gives it only to someone who wants to find it but not use it, so Harry gets it. Quirrell cannot touch Harry because Lily's love for her son left a protection on him. Quirrell dies. Dumbledore awards last-minute points, and Gryffindor wins the House Cup.
