# Book 4: Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

The Weasleys take Harry to the Quidditch World Cup. After the match, Death Eaters, Voldemort's followers, attack, and someone casts the Dark Mark in the sky. At Hogwarts the Triwizard Tournament is announced. It is a contest between three schools: Hogwarts, Beauxbatons from France, and Durmstrang from Bulgaria. Each school picks one champion, and only students over seventeen can enter.

The Goblet of Fire chooses Fleur Delacour, Viktor Krum, and Cedric Diggory. Then it unexpectedly chooses Harry as a fourth champion, even though he is only fourteen. Ron stops speaking to him because he thinks Harry entered himself.

The first task is to get past a dragon. The second is to rescue a hostage from the bottom of the Black Lake. The third is a maze with dangerous creatures. At the Yule Ball dance Harry takes Parvati Patil, and Hermione surprises everyone by going with Viktor Krum.

Harry and Cedric reach the Triwizard Cup at the same time, and it is a portkey that carries them to a graveyard. Cedric is killed on Voldemort's order by Peter Pettigrew. Voldemort is brought back to a body using Harry's blood. Harry fights him in a duel and escapes with Cedric's body.

Back at school Harry learns that Barty Crouch Jr., disguised as Professor Moody, had put his name in the Goblet to deliver him to Voldemort. The Minister of Magic, Cornelius Fudge, refuses to believe Voldemort has returned. Dumbledore warns the school that dark times are coming.
