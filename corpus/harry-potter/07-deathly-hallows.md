# Book 7: Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows

Harry, Ron, and Hermione leave Hogwarts to find and destroy the remaining Horcruxes. Voldemort's Death Eaters have taken over the Ministry of Magic. The Order of the Phoenix moves Harry from the Dursleys' house, and Mad-Eye Moody and Hedwig the owl die in the escape. Harry gives up on school and goes on the run.

The three friends camp in forests, as they have no safe place to hide. They destroy the locket Horcrux, and Ron leaves for a while and returns to save Harry. They learn about the Deathly Hallows, three legendary objects: the Elder Wand, the Resurrection Stone, and the Cloak of Invisibility. Whoever owns all three would be master of death. Harry already has the cloak.

At Godric's Hollow, Harry visits his parents' graves. They are captured and taken to Malfoy Manor, where Dobby helps them escape and dies. Harry learns from Snape's memories that Snape loved Lily Potter and was secretly protecting Harry on Dumbledore's orders. He also learns that he is himself the final Horcrux and must die.

In the Battle of Hogwarts, many defenders die, including Fred Weasley, Remus Lupin, and Tonks. Harry walks into the Forbidden Forest and lets Voldemort strike him down. He wakes in a place like King's Cross station, where he speaks with Dumbledore, and then chooses to return.

Neville Longbottom kills the snake Nagini, the last Horcrux. Molly Weasley kills Bellatrix. Voldemort's curse rebounds and kills him because the Elder Wand does not obey him. An epilogue shows Harry, Ron, and Hermione nineteen years later, sending their children to Hogwarts.
