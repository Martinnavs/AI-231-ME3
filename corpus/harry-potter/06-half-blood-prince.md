# Book 6: Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince

Voldemort is back and the wizarding world is at war. Dumbledore takes Harry on private lessons, using a device called the Pensieve to show him memories about Voldemort's past. They learn that Voldemort split his soul and hid the pieces in objects called Horcruxes so that he cannot die. The diary from the second book was one of them.

Harry finds an old Potions textbook that once belonged to the Half-Blood Prince. Its handwritten notes help him become top of the class, and Professor Slughorn praises him. Harry also uses one of the Prince's spells on Draco Malfoy, nearly killing him. Harry later learns the Half-Blood Prince is Severus Snape.

Harry gets Slughorn to give him a true memory about Tom Riddle asking about Horcruxes. Harry also becomes close to Ginny Weasley, and Ron starts dating Lavender Brown. Meanwhile Draco Malfoy has been given a secret mission by Voldemort and acts strangely. He is repairing a Vanishing Cabinet to let Death Eaters into Hogwarts.

Dumbledore and Harry travel to a sea cave to find a Horcrux, a locket. Dumbledore drinks a poison potion to get it and is badly weakened. Back at school they find that the locket is a fake.

On the Astronomy Tower, Draco cannot bring himself to kill Dumbledore. Snape arrives and kills him instead. Harry chases Snape but is beaten. Dumbledore is buried, and Harry decides not to return to school but to hunt the Horcruxes.
